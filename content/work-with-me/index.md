## Research Areas

My research interests include:

- Infectious disease modeling
- Wastewater-based epidemiology
- Bayesian inference
- Uncertainty quantification
- Inverse problems for partial differential equations
- Statistical modeling for genomic data
- Wind energy systems

## Opportunities

I am open to collaborating with:

- Undergraduate students
- Graduate students (MS/PhD)
- Postdoctoral researchers

## Who Should Reach Out

I encourage students and researchers with backgrounds in:

- Mathematics or Applied Mathematics
- Statistics or Data Science
- Epidemiology
- Computational Biology
- Quantitative Biology

## How to Get in Touch

I am especially interested in working with individuals who are proactive, curious, and excited about interdisciplinary collaboration.

If you are interested in working with me, please email me at [luisa.daza@im.unam.mx](mailto:luisa.daza@im.unam.mx).

When you write, please include:

- A brief introduction
- Your CV
- A short description of your interests
- Optional: project ideas or research questions you would like to explore

## Funding Opportunities

At this time, I am not able to directly provide funding. I strongly encourage prospective students and researchers to apply for external fellowships and scholarships that can support their research and academic development.

Useful programs and resources include:

- Graduate studies in mathematics at Instituto de Matemáticas UNAM: [https://matematicas.posgrado.unam.mx/](https://matematicas.posgrado.unam.mx/)
- Postdoctoral opportunities through DGAPA UNAM: [https://dgapa.unam.mx/index.php/formacion-academica/posdoc](https://dgapa.unam.mx/index.php/formacion-academica/posdoc)
- National postdoctoral fellowships (Mexico) via SECIHTI: [https://secihti.mx/convocatoria/becas-nacionales/convocatoria-2025-1-de-estancias-posdoctorales-por-mexico/](https://secihti.mx/convocatoria/becas-nacionales/convocatoria-2025-1-de-estancias-posdoctorales-por-mexico/)

## Note for Prospective Graduate Students

Students interested in pursuing an MS or PhD should apply directly through the appropriate graduate program and follow all application requirements and deadlines.

I am happy to discuss potential research alignment once you have been admitted or while you are preparing your application.
