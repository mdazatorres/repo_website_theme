I welcome motivated and curious individuals who are interested in contributing to interdisciplinary research at the intersection of mathematical modeling, infectious disease dynamics, and Bayesian inference. My interests are not limited to infectious disease dynamics. I have also worked on inverse problems for PDEs and uncertainty quantification.

My research is driven by applied questions, with experience spanning infectious disease modeling, genomic data analysis, and wind energy systems. I am particularly interested in projects grounded in real data and practical challenges, where mathematical and statistical tools can provide meaningful insights and inform decision-making.

Opportunities for collaboration are open to undergraduate students, graduate students, and postdoctoral researchers.

## Who Should Reach Out

I encourage students and researchers with backgrounds in:

- Mathematics or Applied Mathematics
- Statistics or Data Science
- Epidemiology
- Computational or Quantitative Biology

## To reach Out
 Email me with a brief introduction, your CV, and a short description of your interests. Project ideas are welcome but optional.

I am particularly interested in working with individuals who are proactive and interested in interdisciplinary collaboration.

## Funding Opportunities

At this time, I am unable to directly provide funding. I strongly encourage prospective students and researchers to apply for external fellowships and scholarships, which can support both your research and academic development.

Below are some useful programs and resources:

- Graduate studies in mathematics at Instituto de Matemáticas UNAM: [https://matematicas.posgrado.unam.mx/](https://matematicas.posgrado.unam.mx/)
- Postdoctoral opportunities through DGAPA UNAM: [https://dgapa.unam.mx/index.php/formacion-academica/posdoc](https://dgapa.unam.mx/index.php/formacion-academica/posdoc)
- National postdoctoral fellowships (Mexico) via SECIHTI: [https://secihti.mx/convocatoria/becas-nacionales/convocatoria-2025-1-de-estancias-posdoctorales-por-mexico/](https://secihti.mx/convocatoria/becas-nacionales/convocatoria-2025-1-de-estancias-posdoctorales-por-mexico/)

## Note for Prospective Graduate Students

Students interested in pursuing an MS or PhD should apply directly through the appropriate graduate program and follow all application requirements and deadlines. I am happy to discuss potential research alignment once you have been admitted or are preparing your application.
